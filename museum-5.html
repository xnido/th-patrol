Section 5 · Working Unitedly as Sons of Light

The final part of our museum highlights how Jehovah has blessed the unity among His servants.

Missionaries

Here you'll meet brothers and sisters who devoted entire lifetimes to strengthening the work in Thailand. Missionaries arrived from many lands.

Learning the language wasn’t always easy. Missionary sisters commented how the characters looked like worms or spaghetti! Yet they learned the language, adapted to local culture, and served alongside Thai brothers and sisters. They made Thailand their home.

Local publishers

Local brothers and sisters played a vital role.

Bantoeng Chantraboon, Somsri Darawan, and Asawin Urairat

They received training from Gilead.

They demonstrated remarkable faithfulness and helped train others.

Laotian brothers and sisters

During a time of political turmoil, many our Laotian brothers and sisters became refugees in Thailand. Even in refugee camps, they continued holding meetings, preaching, and helping others learn Bible truth.

They helped some get baptized in the refugee camp.

As Laotian brothers and sisters settled in other countries, they remained steadfast in their faith and continued letting the light of truth shine wherever they went.
