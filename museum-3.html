Section 3 · Shining Through Dark Trials

World War II Begins

By the early 1940s, the preaching work in Thailand was gaining momentum. Then everything changed almost overnight when World War II reached Thailand.

Brothers taking the lead were put in internment camps. Others were arrested, interrogated, or placed under military confinement. The preaching work was banned, literature was confiscated, and communication with world headquarters stopped.

Conditions during the war

Basic items like sugar, matches, and fuel were rationed. Yet the brothers remained spiritually strong, studying older Watchtower magazines when no new literature was available.

Though conditions were tough, faithful single sisters Chomchai and Buakhieo courageously continued serving Jehovah despite arrests, risks, and growing opposition.

What was the result? By the end of the war, instead of shrinking, the number of publishers had grown significantly. Jehovah blessed their endurance.
